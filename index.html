Who we are

This website is run by Arundel Capital Ventures Limited, 21 Priory Office Park, Stillorgan, Co. Dublin, Ireland (VAT registration number 797087). We are the controller of the personal data described here.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) records your IP address and basic browser details in its server logs when you load the site.
  • Fonts: the typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google and shares your IP address with it.

Why we use it

We use what you send through the contact form to reply to you and follow up on your enquiry, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering messages and of steps taken at your request before a possible engagement. Technical data is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Cookies

We do not use advertising, analytics or profiling cookies. The only thing we store on your device is your answer to the cookie notice, so we don't ask again. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your data. It is handled by us and by the providers that host the site and deliver its fonts, some of which may process data outside the European Economic Area under appropriate safeguards. We keep enquiries only as long as needed to deal with them and to meet our legal obligations.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and to restrict or object to its use. To do so, write to us at the address above. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ireland (dataprotection.ie).
